# Within-release (10 x 5-fold CV)

Experiment `main_within_release`.

- Splits: 1700; seeds per learned model: 1; rule-based baselines: 1 (deterministic).
- Models (preprocessing, imbalance): `mlp` (log_standard, class_weight), `lr` (log_standard, class_weight), `rf` (standard, class_weight), `xgb` (standard, class_weight), `manual_up` (none, none), `manual_down` (none, none), `all_defective` (none, none), `majority` (none, none).
- Optuna trials per tuning run: 15 (chosen hyperparameters in `results/<experiment>/hyperparameters.csv`).

Summaries: mean and percentile-bootstrap 95% CI (2,000 resamples) over all splits x seeds. Statistical comparisons use the per-split mean across seeds (each split counts once). `@0.5` = 0.5 threshold; `@tuned` = MCC-maximising threshold chosen on the validation split. Lower is better for Brier, ECE, IFA and NECM15; higher for all other metrics.
