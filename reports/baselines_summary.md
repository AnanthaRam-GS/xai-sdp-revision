MCC, F1, Recall and NECM15 use a 0.5 threshold (results at the validation-tuned threshold are in `results/<experiment>/metrics.csv`). Lower NECM15 is better; for every other metric higher is better.

Baselines: `manual_up` (risk 1 / max(LOC, 1)) and `manual_down` (risk LOC), both min-max normalised on training data; `all_defective` (probability 1.0 for every class); `majority` (DummyClassifier, prior strategy). Baselines always use raw features (no preprocessing). Effort-aware metrics rank by predicted risk density (probability / LOC); a constant predictor (`all_defective`, `majority`) therefore inspects the smallest classes first, i.e. it behaves like the ManualUp effort baseline, whereas `manual_down` inspects the largest classes first.

Per-project CIs under the time-aware protocol rest on only 2-4 release pairs and are indicative only.
