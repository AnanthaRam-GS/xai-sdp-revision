# Trivial baselines

Generated by `python -m scripts.run_baselines`. Values are the mean over splits with a percentile bootstrap 95% CI (2,000 resamples, seed 42). `ALL` pools every split of the protocol. MCC, F1, Recall and NECM15 use a 0.5 threshold (results at the validation-tuned threshold are in `results/<experiment>/metrics.csv`). Lower NECM15 is better; for every other metric higher is better.

Baselines: `all_defective` (probability 1.0 for every class); `loc_ranking` (min-max normalised LOC, fitted on training data); `majority` (DummyClassifier, prior strategy). Preprocessing: `standard`. Effort-aware metrics rank by predicted risk density (probability / LOC); a constant predictor (`all_defective`, `majority`) therefore inspects the smallest classes first, i.e. it behaves like the ManualUp effort baseline, whereas `loc_ranking` inspects the largest classes first (ManualDown).

Per-project CIs under the time-aware protocol rest on only 2-4 release pairs and are indicative only.
